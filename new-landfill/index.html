The proposed new landfill north of Six Mile Road: application materials

GFL's Arbor Hills Landfill, Inc. is seeking approval to put landfill cells on about 250 acres of GFL-owned land north of Six Mile Road, across the road from the current landfill. These are the filings, agency letters and the September presentation that make up the public record so far. Every state and county record opens from the Arbor Hills Case File Mirror, a public copy of the state record.

Discharge and stormwater permit track (EGLE NPDES), outfall to Stream S1

Stream S1 is a perennial headwater stream on GFL-owned land north of Six Mile Road that flows to Johnson Drain and the Rouge River. GFL's plans send the new site's stormwater basins to S1 through a channel near the northwest corner. A new discharge to surface water needs an NPDES permit from EGLE. No NPDES application for an outfall from GFL-owned land north of Six Mile Road is on the public record as of Nov 24, 2026.

What the numbers mean

EGLE gives each online filing a submission number that starts with “HQ” (look them up in EGLE’s MiEnviro portal). Issued permits get different numbers. Several of these filings are for the existing landfill south of Six Mile Road, not the new landfill. Statuses as of Nov 24, 2026.

  • NorthHQQ-YTC1-YG4V6EGLE’s number for GFL’s wetland and stream pre-application for the new landfill, filed Aug 20, 2026. Still a pre-application (In Process); no full permit application and no comment period yet.
  • NorthHQA-WX1H-7R9J4A number GFL printed on its Attachment 1 (dated Aug 3, 2026) for the same new-landfill project. It does not appear in EGLE’s public list of submissions for this site, so HQQ-YTC1-YG4V6 is the number to look up.
  • NorthHQ6-JHZM-XRVGA, permit WRP043450A 2024 application and the permit it produced: 0.032 acre of temporary timber mats to drill one groundwater monitoring well. Not a permit to build a landfill.
  • SouthHQK-4R25-67T36A separate, smaller wetland application on the existing landfill: filling 3.78 acres of “Wetland 1” to remove a PFAS source area from a 2016 fire. Filed Jul 23, 2026; public comment ran Aug 26 to Sep 15, 2026. Not part of the new landfill.
  • SouthHQP-5CHQ-0NBWQThe June 2026 pre-application for that same Wetland 1 cleanup (Completed).
  • SouthMI0045713, HQF-GJG4-ZFTB4MI0045713 is the discharge (NPDES) permit for treated groundwater from the Arbor Hills Remediation Area. HQF-GJG4-ZFTB4 is GFL’s 2026 application to renew it.
  • SouthMIS210766The existing site’s industrial stormwater certificate under the state general permit.
  • Site IDsN2688, WDS 475946N2688 is the landfill’s air-quality site number. WDS 475946 is its solid-waste number, used by EGLE’s Materials Management Division.
