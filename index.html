Write the Washtenaw County Commissioners

The nine County Commissioners help decide the Arbor Hills Landfill plan. A short letter from a constituent carries real weight. Pick the issue you care about most, add a line in your own words, then send it to the county commissioners and state legislators at once, from your own email.

1. Pick your issue

Each option gives you a ready letter with one clear request. The more residents send the same request, the harder it is to set aside. Choose the one that matters most to you.

2. About you

Commissioners weigh letters from the people they represent. Your address shows you are a Washtenaw County resident.

3. Your letter
Add a sentence or two in your own words below. Identical form letters get counted and set aside. One honest, personal reason from a real resident is what actually moves a commissioner. Please keep it brief and civil.
4. Send it

Pick whichever matches how you read email. They all send the same letter to the county commissioners and state legislators, from your own account.

Want to reach the committee that is actually writing these rules? The nine commissioners approve the final plan, but the county's Materials Management Plan Committee is writing the minimum siting criteria now. Only two scheduled committee meetings remain: September 9, and December 9, when it plans to vote to approve the completed plan and send it to the commissioners. You can also write the committee members directly, using the county's own page: Washtenaw County Materials Management Plan Committee: members and emails.
