Write the Washtenaw County Commissioners

The nine County Commissioners help decide the Arbor Hills Landfill plan. A short letter from a constituent carries real weight. This letter points to Bloomberg's national investigation and the site's own monitoring record to ask for independent oversight and automatic public disclosure, not assurances that depend on what the operator chooses to submit.
