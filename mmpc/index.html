Is there a landfill capacity crisis?

The committee has been told there is. The state's own numbers point the other way: counting landfills within 52 miles of the Washtenaw County line, EGLE's FY2025 landfill report leaves Southeast Michigan 16.2 years of space at today's rates.

    Suggested Minimum Siting Requirements changes so far

    Wording proposed for the minimum siting criteria in the Materials Management Plan, and where each stands. Status updates after each meeting.

    • Continuous wellhead monitoring, alerting and public data
      Continuous temperature, oxygen, methane and carbon dioxide monitoring across the gas-collection field, with notice to the County and state within 30 minutes of a reading at or above 145°F.
      ProposedSept. 9, 2026 · See the proposed wording
    • Corrective action and fire response
      Corrective action within 5 days of any reading at or above 145°F, and no new waste within 400 feet of a well at or above 170°F.
      ProposedSept. 9, 2026 · See the proposed wording
    • Required elevated-temperature and smolder-prevention plans
      Two engineer-certified public plans, one for subsurface elevated temperatures and one for smolder and fire prevention, reviewed at least once a year.
      ProposedSept. 9, 2026 · See the proposed wording
